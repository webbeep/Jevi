About Free AI search vs Google AI Mode

Free AI search

Good search should be free.

You ask, and the page is the answer. A chart, a comparison, or the steps. Then you tap the part you actually meant. Answers you can see, adjust, and act on.

What you get for free

ZO is free to try. Signed-out use is a small daily allowance. A signed-in account gets a larger one. The search box is the same one as the homepage, and so are the answers: there is no cut-down free version.

Each answer is written from a fresh web search. ZO reads the results, writes one page that answers the question, and lists the sources it used, numbered, so a figure on the page points back to the page it came from.

Answers in the shape you need

Not every answer should be a paragraph. Ask for a comparison and you get a table. Ask how to do something and you get numbered steps. Ask about a price or a score and the figure comes first, with its source and, for live figures, the time it is from.

From there, keep going on the same page. Tap a row to ask about it, or type a follow-up. ZO keeps the conversation, so a short question like "and with tax?" is about the thing you were just looking at.

Check what you read

AI answers can be wrong. The sources are there so you can check. For health, money, or legal questions, open the source before you act on the answer.

Your questions stay yours

ZO sets only essential cookies. Unless you save an answer or turn on history sync, your questions are not stored in our database. The privacy policy has the details.
