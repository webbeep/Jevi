A Google AI Mode alternative

Good search should be free.

This is the search ZO is built for: you ask, and the page is the answer. A chart, a comparison, or the steps. Then you tap the part you actually meant.

ZO does not claim to outscore Google. It is a different page: the answer is something you can see, adjust, and act on, with the sources beside it.

How ZO is different

Google AI Mode answers in conversation, mostly as text. ZO answers on a page. A comparison is a table you can scan, a how-to is a list of steps, a trend is a chart, and a single figure such as a price comes first, with its source. The layout follows the question instead of the other way round.

The page is also where you continue. Tap a row, a step, or a name to ask about it, or type a follow-up. ZO keeps the conversation, so "when did it open?" is about the place you were just reading about.

Sources you can check

Every answer is written from a fresh web search and lists the pages it used, numbered. Facts on the page point back to those numbers, so you can open the source and check the figure. Live figures, such as a stock price, show the time they are from.

No AI search is right every time, ZO included. For health, money, or legal questions, read the source before you act on the answer.

Free, without the trade-off

ZO is free to use, with a small daily allowance signed out and a larger one signed in. It sets only essential cookies, and unless you save an answer or turn on history sync, your questions are not stored in our database.
