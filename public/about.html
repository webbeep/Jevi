ZO: free AI search with sources

ZO is an AI search app. You ask a question in plain words. ZO searches the web, reads what it finds, and answers on one page, with numbered links to the sources it used.

An answer you can see

The answer takes the shape the question needs. A comparison comes back as a table, a how-to as numbered steps, a trend as a chart, and a person or place as a short profile. If the answer is a single figure, the figure comes first.

Then you can go further from the page itself. Tap a part of the answer to ask about it, or type a follow-up. Follow-ups keep the conversation, so "When did it open?" is about the building you were just asking about.

Sources beside every answer

Each answer lists the pages it was written from, and the facts on the card point back to them with numbers like [2]. That way you can open the page and check the figure yourself. Live figures, such as a stock price, show the time they are from.

AI answers can be wrong. For anything that matters, such as health, money, or legal questions, read the source before you act on it.

Free to use

Good search should be free. You can use ZO without an account, up to a small daily allowance. Signing in with Google gives you a larger daily allowance, and lets you save answers to open again later.

Privacy

ZO sets only essential cookies: one for sign-in and one that runs the free daily limit. Unless you save an answer or turn on history sync, your questions and answers are not stored in our database. Visit counts come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies. The privacy policy has the details.

Who makes ZO

ZO is built by the ZO team. For questions, feedback, or deletion requests, write to ask@zo.fyi.
